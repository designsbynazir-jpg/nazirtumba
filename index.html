About The Event

The Formula 1 Australian Grand Prix returns to Melbourne from March 5th to 8th 2026. As the season opener, this iconic race brings world-class drivers, thrilling speed, and passionate fans together at Albert Park Circuit for one unforgettable motorsport weekend.

The Albert Park Circuit

The Albert Park Circuit combines high-speed straights, technical corners, and beautiful lakeside scenery. Its semi-street design produces dramatic overtakes and unpredictable racing moments every season.

With tight braking zones and fast flowing sectors, the track delivers one of the most exciting race starts on the Formula 1 calendar.

World-Class Fan Experience

Beyond the racing, fans enjoy live concerts, driver appearances, interactive fan zones, and premium hospitality lounges.

From grandstand views to VIP suites, Melbourne offers one of the best atmospheres in global motorsport.
